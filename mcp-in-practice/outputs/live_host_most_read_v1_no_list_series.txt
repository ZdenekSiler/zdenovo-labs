A: **Most read:** "The $0 Startup: How AI Eliminates Your First 3 Hires" (published 2026-07-04), with **215 views**.

**Series where readers drop off:** I couldn't get a full list of your series, so I found these five through search. Two have steep drop-offs:

1. **Building a Robust AI Platform (LLM gateways)**: this is the worst. Views fall at every step: 67 → 43 → 28 → 17. Only **25%** of Part 1 readers reach Part 4, and the biggest single drop is Part 3 → Part 4, which keeps 61%.
2. **Jev in Python**: views go 81 → 64 → **30** → 29. There's a sharp cliff at Part 2 → Part 3 ("Confidence Is a Second Return Value"), which keeps only 47% of readers. Those who get past it mostly stay to the end (36% of Part 1 readers finish).

Both started in October 2026, and the view counts are lifetime totals. Later parts have had less time to collect views, so part of these drops may just be timing.

The other three series hold their readers well:
- **LangChain**: 88% of Part 1 readers reach Part 4.
- **dlt**: Parts 3 and 4 get slightly more views than Part 1.
- **Snowflake**: Parts 2–6 keep about 80–90% of Part 1's views, and Part 7 (the comparison with BigQuery, Redshift and others) gets 187 views, more than double Part 1. It probably draws readers directly.
